Questions

Before you start

What do subcontractors pay?

Nothing. They reply to emails like they do today, and they never need an account.

What happens to our data?

It stays in EU regions, it is never used to train a model, and only your project team can see it. The trust page names every provider.

Can we leave?

Yes. You can export your project data at any time and ask us to delete it.

How does the pilot work?

We set up one project with you, you use it for 60 days, and we talk once a week. Afterwards you choose a plan or stop.
