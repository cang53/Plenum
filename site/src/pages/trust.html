Trust

Rules we keep, in plain words

Written for the person who has to say yes: IT, legal and the works council.

01

Plenum reads only the project address

It sees emails that people CC to the project address. It has no access to anyone's inbox.

02

Every AI change needs a click

Suggestions wait until a person applies them. Nothing is created, changed or sent on its own.

03

Every suggestion shows its source

Each one links to the email it came from, so anyone can check it.

04

No statistics about people

Plenum reports on projects. It does not rank, score or count what one person does.

05

Hosted in the EU

The database, email and AI run in EU regions. The next section names every provider.

06

Your data never trains a model

Customer emails and plans are used to run your projects and for nothing else.

07

Access is checked in the database

Who can see what is enforced in the database itself, and the rules are tested.

08

Subcontractors need no account

They reply by email. They are never charged and never asked to sign up.

09

No cookies on this site

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies.

Data location

Where an email goes

From the sender's mail server into EU regions only. Nothing in this path leaves the EU.
